CyberPhrase Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Summary

CyberPhrase does not collect, transmit, sell, or share user data. Generated passphrases and generator preferences are processed locally on your device and are never sent to the developer or an external service.

Information Stored Locally

CyberPhrase saves generator preferences and theme choice locally on your device.

The web app and Chrome extension use browser local storage. The mobile app uses persistent local application storage. Mobile preferences are stored using AsyncStorage, which is unencrypted and is intended for ordinary preferences, not secrets. CyberPhrase stores generator settings and theme choice there, but never generated passphrases.

You can remove saved preferences by clearing the website or extension storage, clearing the mobile app's data, or uninstalling the app.

Generated Passphrases

Generated passphrases are not saved in browser local storage or persistent mobile storage. A generated passphrase exists temporarily in the application while it is displayed.

CyberPhrase does not transmit generated passphrases to the developer, an external API, or another service.

Clipboard Access

CyberPhrase writes a passphrase to your device's clipboard only when you activate the Copy button. The developer does not receive or have access to clipboard contents.

After copying, the passphrase is managed by your operating system or browser. It may remain in clipboard history until it is replaced, removed, or automatically cleared according to your device settings.

Network Requests and Tracking

CyberPhrase does not require an external API or network request to generate a passphrase. It does not include analytics, advertising, tracking technology, or third-party cookies.

CyberPhrase contains links to external websites. Those websites have their own privacy practices and are only contacted if you choose to follow a link.

Chrome Extension Permissions

The CyberPhrase Chrome extension does not request access to websites, browser tabs, browsing history, or other sensitive browser permissions.

Mobile App Permissions

The CyberPhrase mobile app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files, or other sensitive device information.

Limited Use

Information handled by CyberPhrase is used only to provide its passphrase-generation, clipboard, preference-saving, and theme features.

CyberPhrase complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Information is not transferred to third parties or used for advertising, lending, creditworthiness, or other unrelated purposes.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if CyberPhrase's functionality or data practices change. The updated date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.
