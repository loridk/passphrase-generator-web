CyberPhrase Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Summary

CyberPhrase does not transmit, sell, or share user data. Generated passphrases and generator preferences are processed locally on your device and are never sent to the developer or an external service.

Information Stored Locally

CyberPhrase saves your generator preferences and theme choice in your browser's local storage. This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to the developer.

Generated passphrases are not saved in local storage. A generated passphrase exists temporarily in the application while it is displayed.

Clipboard Access

CyberPhrase writes a passphrase to your clipboard only when you activate the Copy button. The developer does not receive or have access to clipboard contents.

A copied passphrase may remain in your device's clipboard history until it is replaced or removed according to your device settings.

Network Requests and Tracking

CyberPhrase does not require an external API or network request to generate a passphrase. It does not include analytics, advertising, tracking technology, or third-party cookies.

CyberPhrase contains links to external websites. Those websites have their own privacy practices and are only contacted if you choose to follow a link.

Chrome Extension Permissions

The CyberPhrase Chrome extension does not request access to websites, browser tabs, browsing history, or other sensitive browser permissions.

Limited Use

Information handled by CyberPhrase is used only to provide its passphrase-generation, clipboard, preference-saving, and theme features. CyberPhrase complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Information is not transferred to third parties or used for advertising, lending, or other unrelated purposes.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if CyberPhrase's functionality or data practices change. The updated date at the top of this page will identify the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about CyberPhrase or this privacy policy can be sent through Lori DK's website.
